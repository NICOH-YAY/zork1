HOLLOW HOUSE

It is 3:00 in the morning. Your sister June went missing from this house twenty years ago, at 3:33. You are back, and you don't remember driving here.

Built from the Zork I source (Infocom, 1980; MIT license, Microsoft 2025).
Type what you want to do, like open mailbox or go north.

HOW TO PLAY

Type short commands and press Enter. look, n / s / e / w / u / d, open window, take lamp, examine photo, talk to mother, read note, inventory, wait. time tells you the hour and the night.

Every night starts at 3:00. At 3:33 the clock strikes and the house resets. You keep what you learned. The house keeps nothing, except the notes someone keeps leaving in the mailbox.

The dark under the house is not safe without a light. Mother doesn't like you touching things before dinner.

Click, press Enter or press space to skip typing and to move past a dialog box. save and restore keep one saved game in this browser.
